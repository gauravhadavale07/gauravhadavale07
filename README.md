I am an undergraduate researcher focused on **opening the "Black Box" of Large Language Models**. My work treats AI models not as magic, but as **control systems** that can be audited, steered, and debugged.

- 🔭 I’m currently working on **Latent Counterfactuals** (finding the "Anger" circuit in GPT-2).
- 🔬 I’m researching **Safety Asymmetry**: Why it's easier to break models than to fix them.
- 👯 I’m looking to collaborate on **Mechanistic Interpretability** and **Model Steering**.
- ⚡ **Key Insight:** "Safety is not just the absence of toxicity; it is a structural geometric constraint."
