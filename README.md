I am an electronics and telecommunication engineering student passionate about **opening the "Black Box" of Deep Learning**. My projects focus on making AI systems—from Medical Vision models to LLMs—transparent, robust, and safe.

- 🔭 I’m currently auditing **GPT-2's internal reasoning** using Activation Steering.
- 🩻 I previously researched **Shortcut Learning** in Pneumonia Detection models.
- ⚡ **Goal:** To build AI systems that doctors and users can actually trust.
